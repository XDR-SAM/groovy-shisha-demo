CLASSICROCKHOUSE SHISHA.

BOLD. DRIVING.
UNMISTAKABLY BOLD.

Classic Rock brings grape and spice together with a bold, distinctive presence. The fruit provides the foundation, while the spice adds warmth and an aromatic edge.

This is a house shisha blend with an assertive character and an attention to balance. Each note has a part to play, like the layers in a great rock record.

Carefully selected, thoughtfully prepared, and Musically Inspired, Classic Rock is composed for an evening with a little extra energy.
