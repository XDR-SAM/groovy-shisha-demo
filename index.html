02 / MUSICALLY INSPIREDSG-001 · SIDE A

THE MUSIC IS
IN THE MAKING.

Music has always had the power to move us. At Groovy Shisha, that energy sets the tone for the evening.

Our Musically Inspired approach brings together carefully selected house blends and a listening program shaped by country, blues, classic rock, and jazz. Each blend has a playlist, and every record adds something to the room.

It is where our passion for music meets our attention to shisha. From the first selection to the final track, aroma, preparation, lighting, and sound come together to give each session its own distinctive character.

The music is part of the evening, from the first track to the last.

06 / OUR STORYANAHEIM, CALIFORNIA

BUILT ON
MUSIC.

BEFORE THERE WAS SHISHA,
THERE WAS MUSIC.

Groovy Shisha brings together a passion for music, the ritual of preparing shisha, and the pleasure of spending an evening in good company.

A great session has the rhythm of a great record. Each part contributes to the whole, from the blend and its preparation to the lighting, conversation, and selection playing through the room.

That idea is the foundation for Groovy Shisha Lounge.

We approach shisha with an appreciation for tradition, an instinct for composition, and an attention to detail. From selecting aromatic blends to building a listening program, music influences the way we welcome guests and shape the evening.

Rooted in a love of music and shared experiences, Groovy Shisha is a place to find your favorite blend, discover a record, and make time for good company.
